# About How Much Mate

Getting quotes for everyday services can be slow and uncertain. You may spend
days contacting different providers and waiting to hear back, only to receive a
price with no easy way to know whether it represents fair value.

How Much Mate helps solve both problems. It is a community-powered database of
real quotes people have received for services in their area. Before accepting a
quote—whether it is for replacing a light switch, repairing a fence, or another
job—you can see what similar work has cost nearby.

By making local pricing easier to compare, How Much Mate gives customers a
useful reference point, helps them set realistic expectations, and makes it
easier to approach the quoting process with confidence.
